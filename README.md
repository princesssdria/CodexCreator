🌟 Grind & Glow — Student Vitality RPG
Grind & Glow is a gamified productivity and wellness web app designed to help college students stay balanced while managing academic stress.
Instead of traditional habit tracking, this app turns daily life into a role-playing game where your actions directly affect your “brightness” — a reflection of your mental, physical, and academic well-being.

💡 Inspiration:
This project was created from personal experience.
As a college student, I often get tunnel vision while focusing on schoolwork and forget to take care of myself—skipping meals, losing sleep, and ignoring my overall well-being.
Grind & Glow was built as a solution to that problem: a system that encourages balance, self-awareness, and consistency through a fun and interactive experience.

🎮 Features:
🌞 Brightness Points System (based on habits)
🔥 Streak Tracking & Multipliers
🧠 Character Statuses (e.g., Locked In, Burned Out)
⚡ Buffs & Debuffs based on behavior
📊 Progress Bars (Energy, Discipline, Brightness)
✨ Animated feedback for actions and leveling

🧬 Example Gameplay:
Studying increases brightness 📈
Sleeping well gives bonus XP 😴
Doomscrolling reduces stats 📉
Consistency unlocks “Glow Mode” ✨
